# Expense Reimbursement SOP

## When to Use
Use this process for any business-related expense not covered by a corporate card, including home office stipends, wellness reimbursements, travel, and client entertainment.

## Steps
1. Log into the Expense system (linked from the Workday homepage under **Quick Links**).
2. Create a new expense report and select the appropriate category (Travel, Meals, Wellness, Home Office, Training, Other).
3. Attach an itemized receipt for any expense over $25.
4. Add a brief business justification (1-2 sentences).
5. Submit for manager approval.
6. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next bi-weekly payroll cycle, typically within 10 business days.

## Spending Limits (require pre-approval above these amounts)
- Meals: $75/person/day
- Local travel (mileage, rideshare): $150/trip
- Client entertainment: $200/event

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol (except client entertainment with VP approval), personal entertainment, traffic fines, and first-class airfare are not reimbursable.
