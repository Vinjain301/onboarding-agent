# Performance Review Process SOP

## Review Cadence
- **30-day check-in**: Informal conversation between new hire and manager to review onboarding progress and early wins/blockers. Not part of the formal record.
- **90-day review**: Formal review of ramp-up against the 30/60/90-day plan, documented in Workday Performance.
- **Annual review**: All employees participate in the annual cycle each November, including self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback (for roles with cross-functional collaboration).

## New Hire 90-Day Review
The 90-day review evaluates:
1. Progress against onboarding goals and role-specific ramp milestones.
2. Completion of required training and certifications.
3. Integration with the team and cross-functional stakeholders.
4. Manager confirms continued employment status (for roles with a probationary period, if applicable in the employee's location).

## Goal Setting
New hires set initial goals with their manager within the first 2 weeks, documented in Workday under **Performance > Goals**. Goals should align with the role-specific 30/60/90-day plan.

## Feedback Tools
Ongoing feedback can be given anytime through the Workday "Feedback" feature, visible to both the employee and their manager.
