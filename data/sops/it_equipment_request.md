# IT Equipment Request SOP

## Standard New Hire Equipment
Every new hire automatically receives, shipped to arrive by their start date:
- Laptop (MacBook Pro for Engineering/Data/Product roles, Windows ThinkPad for all other roles)
- Monitor, keyboard, mouse (hybrid/remote employees only)
- Headset

## Requesting Additional Equipment
1. Go to the IT Portal (linked from Workday under **Quick Links > IT Requests**).
2. Select "New Equipment Request."
3. Choose the item and provide a business justification.
4. Requests under $200 are auto-approved; requests over $200 require manager approval.
5. Standard delivery is 5-7 business days; expedited shipping is available for an additional cost with VP approval.

## Home Office Stipend
Hybrid and remote new hires can request the $500 home office stipend (per the Remote & Hybrid Work Policy) through the IT Portal within their first 30 days. This is fulfilled via reimbursement, not direct purchase — follow the Expense Reimbursement SOP with category "Home Office."

## Software & System Access
Role-based system access (e.g., GitHub, Salesforce, Adobe Creative Cloud, Tableau) is auto-provisioned based on your Workday job profile within 24 hours of your start date. If access is missing after 24 hours, submit an IT Access Request ticket.

## Returning Equipment
All company equipment must be returned to IT within 5 business days of separation, using the prepaid shipping label provided by IT.
