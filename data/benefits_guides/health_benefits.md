# Health & Welfare Benefits Guide

## Enrollment
New hires have 30 days from their start date to enroll in health benefits through Workday Benefits. Coverage is effective on the first day of the month following your start date. If no election is made within 30 days, you will default to the base PPO plan with employee-only coverage.

## Medical Plans
| Plan | Employee Monthly Cost | Deductible (Individual) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base PPO | $45 | $1,500 | Broad network, no referrals needed |
| High Deductible + HSA | $10 | $3,000 | Company contributes $750/year to HSA |
| Premium PPO | $120 | $500 | Lowest out-of-pocket costs |

## Dental & Vision
Dental and vision coverage are bundled at $15/month combined and cover 2 cleanings/year and an annual eye exam plus allowance toward frames or contacts.

## Life & Disability Insurance
- Basic life insurance (1x annual salary) is provided at no cost.
- Supplemental life insurance can be purchased up to 5x salary during enrollment.
- Short-term and long-term disability coverage is company-paid for all full-time employees.

## Dependents
Spouses, domestic partners, and children up to age 26 can be added as dependents during enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, adoption, loss of other coverage).

## Questions
Benefits questions can be directed to benefits@northlightsystems.example or scheduled with a Benefits Counselor through the HR portal.
