# Wellness & Perks Programs

## Mental Health Support
All employees and dependents have access to 8 free therapy sessions per year through the Employee Assistance Program (EAP), available 24/7 at eap.northlightsystems.example or 1-800-555-0142.

## Fitness Reimbursement
Employees can be reimbursed up to $50/month for gym memberships or fitness class subscriptions. Submit receipts through the Expense Reimbursement SOP with category "Wellness."

## Learning & Development Stipend
Every employee has an annual $1,000 learning stipend for courses, certifications, conferences, or books relevant to their role, approved by their manager.

## Parental Leave
- Birthing parents: 16 weeks paid leave.
- Non-birthing parents/adoptive parents: 8 weeks paid leave.
- Leave can begin up to 2 weeks before the expected due date and must be coordinated with HR at least 30 days in advance when foreseeable.

## Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)
Northlight Systems supports several ERGs (Women in Tech, Pride Alliance, Parents Network, Veterans Network). New hires can join any ERG through the HR portal at any time.

## Commuter Benefits
Pre-tax commuter benefits (transit and parking) are available for onsite and hybrid employees, elected in Workday under **Benefits > Commuter**.
