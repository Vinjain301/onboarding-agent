# 401(k) Retirement Savings Plan

## Eligibility
All employees are eligible to enroll in the 401(k) plan starting on their first day of employment. There is no waiting period.

## Company Match
Northlight Systems matches 100% of the first 3% of contributions and 50% of the next 2%, for a maximum company match of 4% when an employee contributes at least 5% of eligible pay.

## Vesting
- Employee contributions are always 100% vested.
- Company match vests over 3 years: 0% at hire, 50% after year 1, 100% after year 2 of service.

## Contribution Limits
IRS limits apply annually (published on the HR intranet each January). Employees age 50+ are eligible for catch-up contributions.

## Investment Options
The plan offers a range of target-date funds, index funds, and actively managed funds through the plan administrator (Fidelity). A default target-date fund based on estimated retirement year is assigned if no election is made.

## Changing Contributions
Contribution percentage can be changed at any time in Workday under **Benefits > Retirement**, effective the next pay cycle.
