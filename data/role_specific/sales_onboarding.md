# Sales (Account Executive) Onboarding Guide

## Required Training (First 14 Days)
- Security Awareness Training (all employees)
- Sales Bootcamp (1 week, covers product, pricing, competitive positioning, and CRM usage)
- Value Selling Methodology certification (required before running solo customer calls)

## Systems & Access Provisioned Automatically
Salesforce (CRM), Outreach (sales engagement), Gong (call recording/coaching), pricing and quoting tool, Slack sales channels.

## 30-Day Milestones
- Complete Sales Bootcamp and shadow at least 5 customer calls with senior AEs.
- Learn the product demo script and deliver a practice demo to manager.
- Complete Value Selling Methodology certification.

## 60-Day Milestones
- Run first solo discovery call and demo.
- Build a qualified pipeline of at least 10 opportunities.
- Shadow a deal through the full sales cycle with a senior AE as mentor.

## 90-Day Milestones
- Close or materially advance at least 2 opportunities independently.
- Complete 90-day review with manager against quota ramp expectations.
- Fully own an assigned territory or account list.

## Recommended Learning Path
1. Product & Competitive Positioning course
2. Value Selling Methodology certification program
3. Objection handling workshop
4. CRM & pipeline hygiene training (Salesforce)
