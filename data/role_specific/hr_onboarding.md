# HR Coordinator Onboarding Guide

## Required Training (First 14 Days)
- Security Awareness Training (all employees)
- HR Systems Fundamentals (Workday HCM navigation, case management tool)
- Confidentiality & Employee Data Handling training (mandatory before accessing employee records)

## Systems & Access Provisioned Automatically
Workday HCM (HR partner access), HR case management tool, background check vendor portal (view-only initially), Confluence HR space.

## 30-Day Milestones
- Complete HR Systems Fundamentals and Confidentiality training.
- Shadow onboarding, offboarding, and employee relations case processes with a senior HR team member.
- Learn escalation paths for employee relations, benefits, and payroll issues.

## 60-Day Milestones
- Independently process new hire onboarding paperwork and system setup for an assigned employee group.
- Handle first-line employee inquiries with support from senior HR team member.

## 90-Day Milestones
- Fully own onboarding/offboarding processing for an assigned business unit.
- Complete 90-day review with manager against these milestones.
- Begin handling routine employee relations questions independently, escalating complex cases per policy.

## Recommended Learning Path
1. Workday HCM certification (internal, self-paced)
2. Employment law fundamentals (overview relevant to HR Coordinator scope)
3. Employee relations case handling workshop
4. Confidentiality & data privacy refresher (annual requirement)
