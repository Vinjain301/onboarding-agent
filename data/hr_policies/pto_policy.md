# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Accrual
- Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of PTO per month worked (21 days/year), starting from their first day of employment.
- Part-time employees accrue PTO on a prorated basis according to scheduled hours.
- PTO accrual is visible in Workday under **Time Off > Balances**.

## Eligibility to Use PTO
New hires may begin using accrued PTO after completing 30 days of employment, except for previously approved pre-hire commitments (documented during offer negotiation).

## Requesting Time Off
1. Submit a time-off request in Workday at least 5 business days in advance for planned absences.
2. Your manager will approve or deny the request within 2 business days.
3. For unplanned absences (illness, emergencies), notify your manager as soon as possible and log the absence in Workday retroactively.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Any balance beyond that is forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.

## Holidays
Northlight Systems observes 10 paid company holidays per year, published annually on the HR intranet.

## Sick Leave
Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and accrues at 1 day per month, up to 12 days per year, in accordance with applicable state and local law.
