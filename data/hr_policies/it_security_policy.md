# IT Security & Acceptable Use Policy

## Account Setup
All new hires receive a company identity (Okta SSO) activated on or before their start date. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enrolled within the first 24 hours of access.

## Password & Access Requirements
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days for systems not covered by SSO.
- Access to systems follows least-privilege: employees only receive access required for their role, department, and location, provisioned automatically based on the Workday job profile.
- Elevated access (admin rights, production systems) requires manager + system owner approval via the IT Access Request form.

## Device Security
- Company laptops are pre-configured with disk encryption, endpoint detection, and automatic patching. Do not disable these agents.
- Report a lost or stolen device to IT Security immediately at security@northlightsystems.example or via the 24/7 IT hotline.

## Data Classification
- **Public**: Marketing material, job postings.
- **Internal**: Internal docs, SOPs, non-sensitive project data.
- **Confidential**: Employee PII, financial data, client contracts.
- **Restricted**: Legal matters, M&A information, security incident details.

Confidential and Restricted data must never be stored on personal devices, personal cloud storage, or shared outside approved company systems.

## Phishing & Incident Reporting
Report suspicious emails using the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook/Gmail. Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@northlightsystems.example within 1 hour of discovery.

## Required Security Training
All employees must complete Security Awareness Training within their first 14 days and annually thereafter, assigned automatically in the Learning system based on role.
