# Code of Conduct

All employees of Northlight Systems are expected to act with integrity, respect, and professionalism.

## Core Principles
- Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect regardless of role, background, or seniority.
- Avoid conflicts of interest; disclose any outside employment or financial interest that could conflict with company business to your manager and HR.
- Protect confidential company, client, and employee information at all times.
- Report suspected violations of this policy to your manager, HR Business Partner, or through the anonymous Ethics Hotline (ethics@northlightsystems.example).

## Anti-Harassment and Non-Discrimination
Northlight Systems prohibits harassment or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other protected status. Any employee who experiences or witnesses harassment should report it immediately to HR. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is strictly prohibited.

## Use of Company Resources
Company equipment, software licenses, and network access are provided for business purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted as long as it does not interfere with job performance or violate the IT Security Policy.

## Disciplinary Process
Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, following the standard progressive discipline process outlined in the Employee Handbook.

## Who to Contact
- General questions: hr-general@northlightsystems.example
- Ethics concerns: ethics@northlightsystems.example
- Emergency HR escalation: hr-escalations@northlightsystems.example
