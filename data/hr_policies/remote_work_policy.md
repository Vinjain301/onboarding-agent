# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

## Work Location Categories
- **Onsite**: Expected in a company office 5 days/week.
- **Hybrid**: Expected in a company office at least 3 days/week (Tuesday-Thursday core days), remainder remote.
- **Remote**: Primary work location is a home office; occasional travel to a company office may be required for team offsites.

Your work location category is set in Workday under **Job Details > Work Location** and was agreed upon at offer stage. Location changes must be approved by your manager and HR at least 2 weeks in advance.

## Home Office Requirements (Hybrid/Remote)
- Reliable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps download).
- A dedicated, private workspace for calls involving confidential information.
- Company-issued laptop must be used for all work; personal devices are not permitted to access internal systems except through the approved MDM-managed mobile app.

## Core Collaboration Hours
All employees, regardless of location, must be available 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM in their assigned home-office timezone for meetings and synchronous collaboration.

## Equipment
New hires working hybrid or remote receive a one-time $500 home office stipend, requested through the IT Equipment Request SOP within the first 30 days.

## International Remote Work
Working from a country other than your assigned employment country requires prior approval from HR and Legal due to tax and compliance implications. Submit requests at least 4 weeks in advance via the HR portal.
